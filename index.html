常见问题

整理和复制整理有什么不同？

整理会在选定文件夹内移动并分类素材。复制整理会写入另一个目标文件夹并保留来源素材。测试时请使用副本。

为什么设备或日期不正确？

识别依赖文件中可读取的元数据。剪辑或导出可能移除相关信息；缺少拍摄日期时，应用可能使用文件修改日期。

重复文件如何处理？

内容相同的素材会进入相应分类下的重复目录。同名文件另外受跳过、覆盖或重命名策略控制；覆盖会替换已有同名目标文件。

取消能撤销操作吗？

取消停止后续处理，已经完成的复制和移动会保留。

支持哪些语言？

简体中文、繁體中文、English、日本語。

Common questions

How do the two modes differ?

Organize moves files within the selected folder. Copy & Organize copies them to a separate destination while preserving source files. Use media copies when testing.

Why is a device or date incorrect?

Recognition depends on readable metadata. Editing or exporting can remove it. File modification date may be used when capture date is unavailable.

How are duplicates handled?

Identical content is placed in a duplicates folder under the relevant category. Filename collisions separately follow skip, overwrite, or rename settings. Overwrite replaces an existing destination file with the same name.

Does cancellation undo completed operations?

No. It stops further processing; completed copies and moves remain.

Which languages are available?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese.
