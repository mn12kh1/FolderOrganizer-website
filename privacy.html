本地素材处理

FolderOrganizer 在你的 Mac 上扫描和整理你选择的照片、RAW 和视频。应用读取素材类型、拍摄日期、设备信息和文件属性，用于生成分类路径及识别内容相同的文件。素材和这些元数据不会自动上传到开发者服务器。

应用无需账号，不内置广告、第三方广告追踪或开发者远程分析服务。

本地保存的信息

应用在本机保存语言、性能和分类设置。App Store 版本保存系统提供的文件夹授权书签，以恢复你授权的目录访问。

扫描缓存可能包含路径、大小、修改时间、文件标识、内容哈希和整理状态，以减少重复计算。应用也可能在素材库内写入隐藏分类规则记录，供后续识别既有目录使用。这些记录不是素材备份。

本地诊断日志按最多 14 天和约 10 MB 的限制清理。这些限制不适用于扫描缓存和分类记录，后者可能持续保留以支持之后的整理。

你主动发送的反馈

你可以预览、导出诊断报告，或通过系统邮件主动分享。报告可能包含应用及系统版本、处理器架构、性能设置、分类规则、近期诊断事件和你填写的问题描述。自动日志中的素材文件名和完整路径会进行隐藏处理；发送前仍请检查文字和附件。

报告不会自动附带照片、RAW、视频、扫描缓存或素材元数据。发送邮件后，开发者会收到你的发件人地址、文字及附件，仅用于回复、排障和改进软件，不用于广告或营销。客服资料将在问题解决后最多保留 12 个月，再删除；法律要求保留的资料除外。你也可以联系开发者请求提前删除。

邮件传输由你使用的邮件服务处理。开发者无法自动访问你设备中的素材。

TestFlight

使用 TestFlight 时，Apple 可能按其说明、隐私政策及相关设置收集并向开发者提供崩溃、测试活动、设备信息和你提交的反馈。这与应用自身的本地缓存和日志不同。详见 Apple 隐私政策及 TestFlight。

本网站

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有添加广告、分析脚本、追踪 Cookies 或第三方字体。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。详见 GitHub 隐私声明。

你的选择

你决定授权哪些文件夹以及是否发送反馈。退出应用会结束当前文件夹访问。可通过上述邮箱询问删除本地缓存和设置的方法，或请求访问、更正、删除已发送的客服资料。卸载应用不一定会删除素材库中的隐藏记录或全部本地支持文件。

若数据处理方式变化，将更新本政策和生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 7, 2026 · Developer: Xuelei Zou
Contact: bigbearsell@gmail.com

Local media processing

FolderOrganizer scans and organizes photos, RAW files, and videos you select on your Mac. It reads media types, capture dates, camera information, and file attributes to calculate folder paths and identify identical content. It does not automatically upload media or this metadata to a developer-operated server.

The app requires no account and contains no advertising, third-party advertising tracking, or developer-operated remote analytics service.

Local storage

Language, performance, and classification settings are stored locally. The App Store version stores system-provided folder authorization bookmarks to restore access you granted.

The scan cache may contain paths, sizes, modification times, file identifiers, content hashes, and organization status to reduce repeated work. Hidden classification records may also be written in managed media libraries to recognize existing folders. These records are not media backups.

Local diagnostic logs are pruned to limits of 14 days and approximately 10 MB. These limits do not apply to scan caches or classification records, which may remain for later processing.

Feedback you choose to send

You can preview and export diagnostic reports or share them through your mail application. Reports may include app and system versions, processor architecture, performance settings, classification rules, recent diagnostic events, and your problem description. Media filenames and full paths in automatic logs are redacted; please still review all text and attachments before sharing.

Reports do not automatically attach photos, RAW files, videos, scan caches, or media metadata. If you send email, the developer receives your sender address, text, and attachments, used only to reply, troubleshoot, and improve the app, not for advertising or marketing. Support information is retained for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved and then deleted, except where retention is required by law. You may also request earlier deletion.

Your email provider handles delivery. The developer cannot automatically access media on your device.

TestFlight

Apple may collect and share crash information, testing activity, device information, and feedback under its TestFlight information, privacy policy, and relevant settings. This is separate from the app's local logs and caches. See Apple's Privacy Policy and TestFlight.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party fonts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices

You choose which folders to authorize and whether to send feedback. Quitting ends current folder access. Contact the address above for local settings/cache removal instructions or requests to access, correct, or delete support information you sent. Uninstalling may not remove hidden library records or all local support files.

We will update this policy and effective date if our data practices change.
